Airbnb & Short-Stay Changeover Cleans

Changeover cleans for Airbnb hosts, serviced accommodation, holiday lets and short-stay rental properties.

Charged at our standard hourly rate of £65 per hour for a team of 2 cleaners, with a minimum booking of 2 hours.

What Can Be Included

  • Guest-ready cleaning between bookings
  • Kitchen and bathroom reset
  • Beds changed where clean linen is provided on site
  • Bins emptied and floors cleaned
  • Surfaces wiped and presentation checked
  • Fridge checked and obvious issues reported

For Hosts & Property Owners

  • Suitable for Airbnb and short-stay rentals
  • Regular host rates available for repeat bookings
  • Basic issue reporting after cleans where required
  • Restocking checks can be discussed by arrangement
  • Laundry is not included unless agreed separately
  • Fresh linen must be provided on site for bed changes

Airbnb and short-stay changeover cleans are charged hourly to allow for different property sizes, guest conditions and turnaround needs.

Light EOT Property Refresh

Light refresh work is available for end of tenancy cleans only and is designed to improve basic property presentation.

What Can Be Included

  • Basic full-room emulsion where required
  • Glossing where suitable
  • Small touch-ups and minor marks
  • Light filling of suitable screw holes or small wall holes before painting

Important Note

  • Refresh work is quoted separately
  • Available for end of tenancy work only
  • Not suitable for plaster repairs, wallpapering, spraying or specialist finishes
  • Not a full decorating or restoration service
