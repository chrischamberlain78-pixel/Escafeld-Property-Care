Regular Cleans

Our regular cleans are designed to maintain a clean, presentable home on an ongoing basis and are ideal for weekly, fortnightly, or routine visits.

What a Regular Clean Covers

  • General dusting and wiping of everyday surfaces
  • Kitchen worktops, visible fronts, sinks, and main touch points cleaned
  • Bathrooms and toilets cleaned and sanitised
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped where appropriate
  • Bins emptied and finishing touches completed
  • A consistent standard maintained from visit to visit

Best Suited For

  • Homes that are already in decent overall condition
  • Clients wanting regular upkeep rather than a full reset
  • Weekly or fortnightly domestic cleaning visits
  • Maintaining presentation between busier periods
  • Reducing the time and effort needed to stay on top of the home

Regular cleans are intended for maintenance and ongoing upkeep rather than tackling heavier build-up or neglected areas.

Deep Cleans

Our deep cleans are designed to cover the areas people notice most, with extra attention given to build-up, detail work, carpets, and full-property presentation.

All Areas

  • High dusting to remove cobwebs and top-line build-up
  • Skirting boards, frames, handles, switches, and high-traffic touch points cleaned
  • General surfaces dusted and wiped throughout
  • Furniture fronts, visible marks, and presentation areas refreshed
  • Internal window glass cleaned where required
  • Hard floors vacuumed and mopped where appropriate
  • Bins emptied, cleaned, and rubbish removed

Kitchen & Utility

  • Worktops, cupboard fronts, and external appliance surfaces cleaned and degreased
  • Sinks, taps, plug areas, and surrounding surfaces cleaned and polished
  • Microwave cleaned inside and out
  • Inside oven cleaned where requested
  • Inside fridge cleaned where requested
  • Visible marks, splashback areas, and everyday build-up removed
  • Skirting, edges, and accessible corners detailed
  • Floors thoroughly vacuumed and mopped

Bathrooms & Toilets

  • Toilets cleaned, disinfected, and descaled where needed
  • Sinks, baths, showers, taps, and fittings cleaned and polished
  • Soap residue, water marks, and general bathroom build-up tackled
  • Mirrors, visible tile areas, and bathroom touch points cleaned
  • Shower screens, surrounds, and accessible tracks detailed
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped, with bins emptied and disinfected

Carpet Cleaning

  • Professional carpet cleaning available as part of deep cleans and reset cleans
  • Suitable for bedrooms, lounges, hallways, stairs, and high-traffic areas
  • Pre-spray treatment to help break down dirt, marks, and traffic lane build-up
  • Agitation to work the cleaning solution into the carpet fibres
  • Extraction cleaning to remove suspended soil, moisture, and residue
  • Extra drying passes carried out to help reduce drying time where possible

Exact tasks can vary depending on the size, condition, and requirements of the property. If there is something specific you want included, just ask when requesting your quote.
