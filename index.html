Regular cleans are ideal for keeping your home fresh and under control on a weekly, fortnightly, or routine basis.

What We Cover

  • Kitchens, bathrooms, toilets, floors, and touch points
  • Bleach-free, eco-conscious products available on request for suitable areas and surfaces

Deep Cleans

Deep cleans are for homes that need more attention than a regular clean, with extra focus on build-up, detail work, and presentation.

Whole Home Detail

  • High dusting, cobweb removal, skirting boards, frames, handles, and switches
  • Surfaces, furniture fronts, internal glass where required, and hard floors
  • Touch points, edges, corners, bins, and general presentation areas

Kitchen & Utility

  • Worktops, cupboard fronts, sinks, taps, splashbacks, and appliance fronts
  • Microwave, oven, and fridge cleaned where requested
  • Degreasing, visible build-up removal, and floor cleaning

Bathrooms & Toilets

  • Toilets, sinks, baths, showers, taps, mirrors, and fittings cleaned
  • Soap residue, water marks, descaling, and bathroom build-up tackled
  • Bleach-free product options available where suitable
  • Floors cleaned, bins emptied, and touch points sanitised

Exact tasks can vary depending on property size, condition, and requirements. If there is something specific you want included, including bleach-free product preferences, just ask when requesting your quote.
